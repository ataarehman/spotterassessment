# Loom outline (5–10 minutes)

1. Enter Dallas to Chicago, a departure time, a load above 40,000 lb, and 25-mile spacing.
2. Show three GraphHopper alternatives on the map and the recommended card.
3. Open one checkpoint and compare its ETA with the forecast hour that was sampled. Show that a later checkpoint uses a later hour.
4. Change the load across 30,000 and 40,000 lb and point at the wind rule that changes No Travel or Severe.
5. Sort explanation: Severe miles, then High miles, then average risk, then duration. Mention No Travel routes sort after clean ones.
6. Move the 0–48 hour slider and switch wind, rain, and snow without the map being recreated.
7. Show the error returned when GraphHopper does not provide three alternatives.
8. State that this account cannot use the truck profile, that routing is GraphHopper `car`, and that the app is local at http://127.0.0.1:8080 rather than a public host.
